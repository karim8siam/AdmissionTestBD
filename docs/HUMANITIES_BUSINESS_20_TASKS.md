This plan prevents inflated question counts and unsupported past-paper labels. Each content task is released only after its own checks pass.

1. Foundation: official target map, reference-book index, source archive, separate UI, derived coverage counters, and review gate.
2. Humanities Bangla syllabus map and past-paper benchmark analysis.
3. Humanities Bangla chapter banks: 100 original MCQs per verified chapter/topic.
4. Humanities English syllabus map and past-paper benchmark analysis.
5. Humanities English chapter banks: 100 original MCQs per verified chapter/topic.
6. Humanities general knowledge and analytical-ability banks with dated-current-affairs handling.
7. Humanities past-paper archive: source, institution, unit, session, set/shift, completeness and answer-key verification.
8. Humanities 100-question paper blueprint and criticism-engine calibration against verified papers.
9. Humanities model tests 1-50 with explanation, exposure and method-variety checks.
10. Humanities model tests 51-100 and 2,000 distinct knowledge points.
11. Business Accounting syllabus map and benchmark analysis.
12. Business Accounting chapter banks with calculation and error-analysis coverage.
13. Business Organization and Management map and chapter banks.
14. Business Finance, Banking, Marketing and Production chapter banks.
15. Business Bangla and English admission banks.
16. Business analytical-ability and quantitative banks.
17. Business past-paper archive with source and answer verification.
18. Business 100-question paper blueprint and criticism-engine calibration.
19. Business model tests 1-100 and 2,000 distinct knowledge points.
20. Full content audit, teacher-review workflow, regression test and final release report.

## Publication gate

A question must have four distinct options, one supported answer, an explanation, a distinct method/family tag, a reference mapping and an independent review status. A model paper must contain exactly 100 questions, no duplicate text or IDs, at least 80 method families, no family more than twice, a checked subject distribution and a complete benchmark comparison. Across a 100-paper series, an exact question may appear at most five times. Past-paper entries require a traceable source and must preserve the institution, unit, session, shift/set and actual format. Missing papers remain missing.
